# Privacy Policy — RICK STUDIO

Last updated: October 18, 2026

RICK STUDIO respects your privacy.

RICK STUDIO is a productivity application designed to help users manage their TikTok accounts and create, schedule, and publish content.

## Information We May Process

RICK STUDIO may process information necessary to provide its services, including TikTok account information, authorization tokens, profile information, and content uploaded or created by the user.

## TikTok Account Information

TikTok account access is provided through TikTok's official authorization system. RICK STUDIO does not ask users to provide or share their TikTok password directly with the application.

## Use of Information

Information is used only to provide and improve RICK STUDIO features, including account management, content creation, scheduling, and publishing.

## Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and authorization credentials from unauthorized access.

## Third-Party Services

RICK STUDIO may interact with third-party services such as TikTok and OpenAI when required by features enabled by the user. Their respective privacy policies may also apply.

## Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their RICK STUDIO data by contacting the application owner.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact the RICK STUDIO application owner.
